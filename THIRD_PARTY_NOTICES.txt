WarbandUI.dll: third-party notices

WarbandUI.dll is part of Warband by Seth Summers.

Linked into the dll are two open-source libraries: CommonLibSSE NG (MinLL/CommonLibVR) and spdlog.
Used through an interface header, with nothing of theirs linked in but what such a header holds: Magelight UI
(MagelightUI_API.h), Prisma UI (PrismaUI_API.h) and SKSE Menu Framework (SKSEMenuFramework.h, whose declarations
follow Dear ImGui's interface). The notices and licence texts that belong to these follow, in that order:
CommonLibSSE NG, spdlog, the three interface headers, Dear ImGui.
